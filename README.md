# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes pizza sales data to understand overall business performance, revenue trends, customer ordering patterns, pizza category performance, pizza size preferences, and the performance of individual pizza products.

The goal of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful and actionable business insights using data cleaning, data modeling, DAX, and interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall pizza sales performance
- Identify sales and revenue trends
- Understand customer ordering patterns
- Identify the best-performing pizza categories
- Analyze pizza size preferences
- Identify top-performing pizzas
- Identify low-performing pizzas
- Compare revenue and quantity sold
- Generate useful business insights from sales data

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Overview

The first dashboard provides an overview of the pizza business performance.

It includes:

- Total Orders
- Total Pizzas Sold
- Total Revenue
- Average Order Price
- Average Pizzas per Order
- Orders by Day
- Orders by Month
- Revenue by Pizza Category
- Revenue by Pizza Size
- Pizza Quantity by Category

---

### 2. Pizza Performance Analysis

The second dashboard focuses on individual pizza performance.

It includes:

- Top Pizzas by Revenue
- Top Pizzas by Quantity Sold
- Top Pizzas by Total Sales
- Bottom Pizzas by Revenue
- Worst Performing Pizzas by Quantity
- Pizza-level performance comparison

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- Orders are highest toward the **weekend**, particularly on **Friday and Saturday**.
- **July and January** show high order volumes compared with other months.
- The **Classic pizza category** contributes the highest share of total sales.
- **Large-size pizzas** contribute the highest revenue among pizza sizes.
- A small group of pizzas contributes significantly to overall revenue.
- Some pizzas have relatively low sales and may require promotional strategies or menu optimization.
- Revenue and quantity analysis can help identify products that drive overall business performance.
